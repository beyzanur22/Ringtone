Child Safety Standards

Effective Date: June 30, 2026

At Musica, the online safety of children is our highest priority. This document describes our standards and the measures we take against child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE). It complies with the Google Play Child Safety Standards policy.

1. Zero Tolerance Statement

Musica maintains a zero tolerance policy toward any child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) content. The creation, upload, sharing, searching, or distribution of such content through our application is strictly prohibited.

2. Our Standards

  • The app is not a social platform where users can upload and share content; it is designed for searching and listening to music and ringtones.
  • No CSAE related content, search, or behavior is permitted, and it is blocked immediately when detected.
  • Accounts or devices that share or attempt to access such content are blocked.
  • Any detected CSAE material is reported to the relevant authorities and, where required, to national centers in accordance with applicable laws.

3. Reporting Mechanism

You can report any content or behavior that violates child safety through the app or through this page. All reports are kept confidential and reviewed as soon as possible.

  • Direct contact for reporting: ackgzweb@gmail.com
  • Including the date, a screenshot, and relevant details in your report helps speed up the review.

4. Compliance with Laws

Musica is committed to complying with applicable child safety laws and regulations in the regions where it operates. If CSAE material is detected, we cooperate with the relevant law enforcement agencies and authorized child safety organizations in line with our legal obligations.

5. Continuous Improvement

We regularly review our child safety standards and measures, update them according to new risks and regulations, and make the necessary improvements.
