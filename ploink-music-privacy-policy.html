Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 4, 2026

Application: Ploink : MP3 & Sounds  ·  Package name: com.ploink.app

Welcome to Ploink. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Ploink : MP3 & Sounds (package name: com.ploink.app). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application. Ploink lets you discover ringtones, alarm sounds, notification sounds and music, play them, save them to your device, and set them as your ringtone.

2. Information We Collect

Information you provide:

  • Search queries: When you search for ringtones or music, your search terms are processed to deliver results. We do not permanently store your search history on our servers.
  • Playlists and favorites: Your playlists and favorite tracks are stored locally on your device.
  • Downloaded content: Downloaded files are stored locally in your device storage.
  • Backups: If you use the backup feature, your playlists, downloads and settings are written to a single backup file (.rmbackup) in your device Downloads folder. This file is not uploaded to our servers; it remains only on your device, and you decide whether to move or share it.
  • Feedback: If you send us feedback through the app, the message you write is stored on our servers so we can read and act on it.

Information collected automatically:

  • Device information: General device information (Android version, device model) may be collected for compatibility purposes.
  • Anonymous device identifier, IP address and country: Requests from the app carry an anonymous device identifier. It is used to count active users, apply rate limits, and protect the service from abuse. It is not linked to your name, email address, or any account.
  • Notification tokens: If you enable notifications, your device token is collected through OneSignal so we can send you updates.
  • Usage data: Anonymous usage statistics are collected through Google Firebase Analytics to improve app performance and understand which features are used.
  • Advertising data: Our app may display ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your advertising ID (Advertising ID), your IP address, and general device and usage information to serve, personalize, and measure ads. You can reset your advertising ID from your device settings or turn off personalized ads.

We do not ask for and do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, photos, or microphone input. The app does not require you to create an account.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the ringtone and music service
  • To deliver search results and stream audio and video content
  • To let you download sounds and set them as ringtone, alarm, or notification tone
  • To send notifications about new features (with your consent)
  • To improve app performance and user experience
  • To cache content for faster playback

4. Data Storage and Security

  • Playlists, favorites, recently played, downloaded files, and backup files are stored locally on your device and are not uploaded to our servers.
  • Secure HTTPS connections are used for all network communication.
  • Content may be temporarily cached for faster playback and lower data usage; the cache is cleared automatically.
  • Industry standard security measures are used to protect our services and your data.

5. Third Party Services

The app uses the following third party services:

  • OneSignal: For notifications. OneSignal Privacy Policy: https://onesignal.com/privacy_policy
  • Google AdMob: To display ads. Google privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Firebase Analytics: For anonymous usage statistics. Google privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Play In-App Review: To show the rating dialog inside the app. Google privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties. Exceptions:

  • When required by legal process
  • To protect our rights, privacy, or safety
  • With service providers that help operate the app (under confidentiality agreements)

7. Children Privacy

The app is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

8. Your Rights

You have the following rights:

  • You can delete your locally stored data at any time through the app or by clearing the app data
  • You can disable notifications from your device settings
  • You can request to learn what data we hold about you
  • You can request deletion of data associated with your device

9. Permissions

  • Internet and Network Access: Required to search for and stream media content.
  • Storage and Audio Media (READ_MEDIA_AUDIO): Required to save and read downloaded sound files and backup files on your device.
  • Notifications: Optional, to receive updates about new features.
  • Modify system settings (WRITE_SETTINGS): Optional, requested only when you choose to set a sound as your ringtone, alarm, or notification tone. It is used solely for that action and is never used to change any other system setting. You can decline, and the rest of the app keeps working.
  • Foreground service (media playback): Required to keep playback running and show playback controls in the notification area while the app is in the background.
  • Battery optimization: Optional, requested so that playback is not interrupted when the screen is off; you can decline.

10. Data Retention

Locally stored data remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app. Temporary cache content is cleared automatically. Presence records used for the active user count are removed automatically after 1 hour.

11. Policy Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Significant changes will be announced through the app. Continuing to use the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.

12. Data Deletion Requests

Ploink does not require an account, so there is no account to delete. Data that is stored on our servers is linked only to an anonymous device identifier. You can have that data deleted in either of the following ways:

Option 1 — Delete it yourself (immediate):

  • Uninstalling the app, or clearing the app data from Android Settings → Apps → Ploink → Storage, removes all locally stored data (playlists, favorites, downloads, cache).
  • Turning off notifications in your device settings stops the collection of your notification token.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID from Android Settings → Google → Ads.

Option 2 — Ask us to delete server-side data:

  • Send an email to bnguzellik22@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request".
  • We will delete the records associated with your device, including the device identifier, IP address and country records, feedback you submitted, and diagnostic logs.
  • We respond to deletion requests within 30 days. No account or personal document is required to make a request.

Temporary data (presence records, cached content) is deleted automatically without any request: presence records are removed after 1 hour, and cached media content is cleared automatically.
